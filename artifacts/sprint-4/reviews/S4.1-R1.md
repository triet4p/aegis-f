## 3. Detailed Verification Findings

### 3.1 Provenance and Model Integrity

Upstream model and export repositories were cross-referenced against commit hashes and primary cards:
- Checkpoint: `https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya` at revision `7b928d828b7b0e022f929d9bd2e44165aa270148`. ModernBERT-large backbone (421M total parameters), Apache-2.0 license.
- ONNX export: `https://huggingface.co/receptron/laya-onnx` at revision `68f27dfe5a27a54fb2b1fefc432f43f972e90868`. Export code Apache-2.0 / MIT.
- The 5 bundle files in the ambient download cache (`F:\llms\hf\models\hub\models--receptron--laya-onnx\snapshots\68f27dfe5a27a54fb2b1fefc432f43f972e90868`) were hashed directly. The file sizes and SHA-256 hashes match `tools/laya/PROVENANCE.md` character-for-character.
- The export tokenizer `tokenizer/tokenizer.json` was verified byte-identical to the checkpoint tokenizer.

### 3.2 Graph Architecture & Decision Head Contract

Direct examination of `laya.onnx` with `onnx` and `onnxruntime` verified:
- Opset 18, single domain. 1842 nodes across 39 distinct standard CPU operators.
- External weight layout: 242 initializers, with 203 stored externally in `laya.onnx.data` (1.68 GB).
- I/O tensors:
  - `input_ids`: int64 `[batch, seq]`
  - `attention_mask`: int64 `[batch, seq]`
  - `marker_pos`: int64 `[batch, options]`
  - `marker_mask`: bool `[batch, options]`
  - `qtype`: int64 `[batch]`
  - `logits`: float32 `[batch, options]`
  - `act_probs`: float32 `[batch, 2]`
- Decision head contract: The model is not a standard generative LM or basic text classifier; it evaluates candidate options at marker positions `[MASK]` through a 2-layer transformer decision head over type-embedded encoder states.

### 3.3 Reference Runner and Upstream Parity

Fresh independent execution of `tools/laya/reference_runner.py` confirmed:
1. Fresh execution produces output bit-for-bit identical to `artifacts/sprint-4/workers/S4.1-A1-smoke.json`.
2. Parity against `@receptron/laya` `Laya.load().systemOne`:
   - `Monthly rent`: Housing 0.9796 (confidence 0.9336) — bit-identical on all 6 options.
   - `Lunch at a cafe, 45000 VND`: unknown 0.7792, Food 0.1553 (confidence 0.5822) — bit-identical on all 6 options.
   - `Payment to Alex`: unknown 0.8532, Fun 0.0525, Food 0.0362 (confidence 0.6445) — bit-identical on all 6 options.
3. Behavior on boundary cases:
   - `Dinner [MASK] Food at night market`: literal `[MASK]` was scrubbed, preventing marker collision; Food was selected with probability 0.7693.
   - Empty description: safely resulted in unknown (confidence 0.3973).
   - Long state: correctly truncated to fit within `max_len = 512` while preserving option markers intact.
