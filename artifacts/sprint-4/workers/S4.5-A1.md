### Outcome

- **Status:** `implementation_complete` for worker scope; Main-owned review/checkpoint gates and user-only physical-device observation remain pending.
- **Observable result:** Added a process-owned `LayaInferenceLifecycle` exposed by `AppContainer`. A single worker owns open, use, and release of the runtime; it allows one active request and one replaceable latest-pending request. Queued cancellation removes that request, superseded work receives an explicit outcome, and cancellation after ORT starts withdraws result delivery without interrupting or claiming to stop native execution. Active work keeps its session until return; `releaseSession()` safely closes and allows reopening, the idle window releases the session/worker, and `close()` rejects new work while deferring active-session release. ORT open/probe/run/close share serialization. The existing debug parity probe now exercises this lifecycle. Notification callbacks do not reference or retain the model; intake remains on its independent IO and Room path. No suggestion UI, correction persistence, notification AI, or stale-form integration was added; those remain S4.6–S4.8 scope.
- **Memory/cancellation ownership:** The queue is a single pending slot, not an accumulating executor queue. Cancelled/superseded pending requests are released; an active request can retain only the inputs required by its current synchronous call and cannot publish after cancellation. Session close occurs on the owning worker after native work returns. Idle sessions are released after 30 seconds or by explicit release; later requests reopen. The lifecycle stores no Activity or notification object and adds no shared result callback.
- **Remaining criteria/blocker:** No device command, install, launch, or physical phone interaction was run (strictly user-only). Android-native execution plus actual typing/navigation and natural-notification concurrency therefore remain unobserved. A copy-ready user-only procedure using the final debug APK and existing parity probe is in `docs/developer-setup.md` §6. Main's independent evidence review and checkpoint are also not worker-owned.
