Steps, with the exact labels the app shows:

1. **Setup and status.** From the home screen (**Aegis F**), tap **Bank notifications**. On that page check the **STATUS** block reports access granted and **Listener connected**, and under **BANK APPS** that your bank package is listed. If the system switch cannot be turned on, use **Open app info** and the phone's own restricted-settings menu — never disable a banking protection. If access is granted but the listener is disconnected, tap **Ask the system to reconnect** once and wait for the status to change by itself. 2. **Natural delivery (the one check no fixture can replace).** Do not trigger a money transfer to test. Wait until the bank app posts a real transaction notification on its own (your next genuine transaction, or an existing one the bank re-posts), then open **Review bank drafts**. Write down whether the bank notification's own text matches the supported shape (`VietinBank: dd/MM/yyyy HH:mm`, `GD: …`, optional `ND: …`) and, without copying the body, note which Android field carried which line (title / text / big text) and whether the package name matches the one listed.
3. **Inspect one draft.** Tap **Review draft #N** on the pending row. The detail shows **Source package**, **Stated date/time**, **Stated amount**, **Bank direction**, **Stated memo**, and **Review status: Pending**. Confirm the 50,000-style debit amount is the transaction amount and not the balance, and that a masked memo still reads as unresolved rather than as a description.
4. **Confirm once.** Correct **Amount (VND)**, **Date**, **Time**, or **Description (optional)** if needed, choose **Expense**, **Income**, or **Internal transfer** explicitly, choose **Category** or **No category**, then tap **Confirm transaction** once. Expected: the detail shows **Linked transaction: #N** and the home screen's **THIS MONTH** card (**Expenses**) increases by exactly that amount once. A second tap must not add a second record.
5. **Dismiss.** On another pending draft tap **Dismiss draft** → **Dismiss draft #N?** → confirm. Expected: **THIS MONTH** does not change because of it.
6. **Possible duplicate.** When the bank posts again under the same notification key with identical amount/minute/memo, the row shows the **Possible duplicate. Both drafts are kept…** note and **View draft #N — Pending/Confirmed/Dismissed**. Both drafts must stay; neither is merged away.
7. **Equal-value, separate transactions.** When two genuinely different transactions of the same amount appear, both rows must appear as separate drafts (the later one flagged as a possible duplicate, still reviewable). If the real notification shows any transaction reference line, note the exact label — that is new format evidence (no reference is parsed today).
8. **Rejections (observe only).** As they arrive naturally, confirm that an OTP/verification-code notification, a security alert, a group summary, a notification from any app not listed under **BANK APPS**, and an empty or fully redacted bank notification produce **no** new row in **Review bank drafts** and change no total.
9. **Revocation and manual use.** Revoke Aegis F's notification access in system settings, reopen **Bank notifications**: the status must say access is revoked (disconnected because the grant is gone). Then post/observe a bank notification and confirm no new draft appears, while **Record a transaction** still saves normally.
10. **Restart, screen lock, and OEM behavior.** Lock the screen and unlock it and confirm nothing is lost or duplicated; force-stop the app (**Settings → Apps → Aegis F → Force stop**) or reboot the phone and reopen it: pending drafts and confirmed records must persist, a confirmed/dismissed draft must never return to pending, and the **THIS MONTH** figure must be unchanged.
11. **Reconnect after a system-side disconnect.** With access still granted, if the status shows a disconnected listener, tap **Ask the system to reconnect** once and verify only notifications still showing on the phone can become drafts; anything already dismissed must not come back.
12. **UI sanity.** Confirm **Review bank drafts** opens without a crash in portrait, in dark theme, and at a larger system font, and that rows/labels are not cut off.

Report, at minimum: whether a real notification arrived naturally, the observed field mapping, whether exactly one ledger record and one **THIS MONTH** change resulted, whether replay/reconnect left totals unchanged, whether rejections stayed silent, whether revocation and restart behaved as above, and any OEM/OS message that blocked a step (quote the message, never the notification body).
