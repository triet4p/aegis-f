## Observed failures and resolutions

Two genuine product defects were found by rendered phone interaction and fixed:

1. The Edit interpretation button shared a narrow action row with Browse drafts/Record manually and was clipped/unavailable on a phone viewport. It is now a separate full-width row; `harness-link-card-bounds.png` shows the rendered action after the fix.
2. After Attach, the shared command changed the stored link but the event-card query effect did not observe the parent refresh key, so the card could show stale link state and omit Unlink. The card now observes that refresh key; `08b-attached-link-visible-and-unlink-ready.png` shows the attached link and working Unlink affordance.

The following were harness/navigation issues, not product failures:

- An early driver tried Edit while event #2 was actively linked. The UI correctly hides editing while attached; the driver explicitly unlinked before the stale-edit scenario.
- After the conflict dialog succeeded and preserved input, the driver expected a nonexistent “Close and re-read” label; the actual action is “Cancel”. Canceling and searching the refreshed card produced screenshot 12 and the stale event snapshot.
- Initial source-card navigation used the wrong scroll direction for an off-viewport UI node. No event/capture was duplicated; the continuation used the already-open event panel and verified the visible conflict card.
- The first Manual screenshots passed accessibility-text checks but visual inspection showed provenance offscreen. Those mis-scrolled captures were removed and replaced with screenshots 13–14 after actual phone swipes; screenshot 13 shows amount/description, and screenshot 14 shows the provenance and explicit-save notice. After Cancel, the app correctly returned to the Manual destination rather than the Chat composer; the final continuation navigated through Chat and restored the synthetic transaction.
- The first post-save screenshot contained an unrelated personal Messenger notification banner. That capture (`04-manual-explicit-save-confirmed.png`) was deleted and is not cited; screenshot 15 is the safe replacement. `results.json` is a raw historical log and still records those then-existing capture paths; the final evidence set above supersedes them.

No unresolved product or verification failures remain. The SDK XML message is a toolchain warning only.
