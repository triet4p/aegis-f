## Real defects versus harness/setup observations

- **Product defects fixed:** narrow-shell bottom-tab label clipping and wrapped Review badge at font scale 1.3; Manual keyboard pan causing toolbar/body to move under the status bar. Both were observed on the rendered device before change and re-proved after change. The keyboard root cause was independently confirmed by window soft-input mode changing from `adjust=pan` to `adjust=resize`.
- **Harness/input quirks, not product defects:** the fixture broadcast did not seed until the isolated app was foregrounded; an initial event reading inserted at the cursor into copied source text and was corrected before saving; Vietnamese Telex transformed one later `Lunch` probe to `Lunch lộ`, which was reverted and not used as model evidence. The successful model review used the separately verified exact `Lunch` input. A later launch capture showed only system splash; it is not counted as app loading.
- **Privacy cleanup:** an accidental home/launcher capture and a personal heads-up-notification capture were deleted immediately and are absent from the evidence listing. After uninstall, a hierarchy-only probe landed on the launcher; its temporary `/sdcard/s68-window.xml` was immediately removed and no launcher screenshot was retained or cited. No DocumentsUI/storage-account listing was opened.
