## What was implemented

- **Spending · confirmed**, **Income · confirmed**, **Internal transfers · confirmed**, **Pending · not counted** and **What these figures cover** cards on the Dashboard, all read through the shared exact query surface (`observeConfirmedLedgerAggregate`, `observeLedgerRecords`, `observeCategory`, `observeDraftCandidateCount`). No second summary, no chart, no new dependency.
- **A selected date range**: two inclusive `YYYY-MM-DD` day fields in the device's zone, both required, resolved into one closed half-open range; an unfinished or reversed range shows no figure and says why.
- **A category filter** (stable id, archived categories included) that narrows the **spending** figure and the breakdown; the income and transfer cards state that they cover every category of the range.
- **Drill-down**: every card opens the records behind it — the list is that card's own scope, states its scope line and its exact total, and a row opens the record's detail view; back returns to the list and then to the Dashboard with the scope intact. The scope lives in the shell, so it survives a destination switch, a route push and process death.
- **Truthfulness**: no draft, transfer or income amount enters a spending figure; the pending card counts the waiting notifications and the unconfirmed records of the range separately; the coverage card says the figures are not a complete bank history.
