## 3. Decisions

1. **The category filter narrows spending only.** The shared aggregate has no per-category breakdown of income or transfers, and inventing one would mean a second, private total. The income and transfer cards therefore state that they cover every category of the range, and the category picker says the same.
2. **Both day ends are required.** The shared aggregate counts a *closed* range, so an unfinished or reversed range shows no figure at all and says why, instead of quietly widening.
3. **The breakdown follows the figure.** With a category chosen, the breakdown lists that category's bucket only, so every number on the Dashboard belongs to the stated scope. Changing the category is done through the picker in the scope card.
4. **Pending is counted, never summed.** The pending card shows how many bank notifications are waiting and how many unconfirmed records fall in the range; no draft amount is added to any figure.
5. **The scope lives in the shell**, next to the Manual list filters, so it survives a destination switch, a route push, and process death.
6. **The old month-summary card was deleted** rather than kept beside the new cards: two competing Dashboard figures would be a scope disagreement waiting to happen.
7. **No chart was added** — S6.3 owns charts and drill-down-by-segment.

## 6. Driver defects versus product defects

Every failure below was diagnosed against the rendered phone surface; **none of them was a defect in the product**.

| Symptom | Owner | Diagnosis | Fix in the driver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `input text` dropped or accented characters ("bus" → `bú`, "taxi" → `tãi`, "transfer" → `tràner`) | driver / phone keyboard | The phone's keyboard is in a Vietnamese mode that composes a vowel with a following letter into an accented vowel. The app stored and displayed exactly what it received. | The driver reads every field back and retries; the seeded markers avoid those pairs; `set_field` refuses to continue when the field does not hold what was asked for |
| The "Add a bank app" dialog never opened | driver | The tap went to the clickable ancestor's box centre, 14 px below the label | `find()` now targets the node that carries the label |
| The archive landed on the wrong category (`S62 Food (archived)`) | driver | The driver tapped the first `Archive` control on the screen | The driver now finds the `Archive` control on the named row (same line, to the right of the name) |
| A screen dump came back empty (`ParseError`) | driver | The dump was taken while the screen was moving | `dump()` retries until it holds a readable tree |
| A tap did nothing and later text was missing | driver | A Google Play services update dialog (`Thông báo cập nhật`, in Vietnamese) had popped over the app and swallowed the taps | The driver closes a system notice before looking again |
| The app left the screen during a field entry | driver | A `Back` key was sent while no keyboard was open, so the app took it and left | `dismiss_keyboard()` sends `Back` only when the keyboard is shown |
| "4 confirmed expenses" reported missing although it was on screen | driver | A screen dump only holds what is on the glass; the check assumed the whole page was dumped | The checks now read the page from the top to the bottom and collect every word |
| 10 unit tests failed with `SQLiteCantOpenDatabaseException` | build configuration, not product | The temporary `applicationIdSuffix` moved Robolectric's data directory | The suffix was reverted; with it reverted the whole suite passes |

Hypotheses tried and rejected: that the missing figures were an app bug (they were present in the dumps and in the screenshots); that the mangled text was an encoding bug in the driver (the app stored exactly the text it received, and the mangling followed the keyboard's accent rules); that the 10 test failures were caused by the new dashboard code (they are in the data-layer migration tests and vanish with the suffix reverted).

The driver's "close the keyboard" step sends that `Back`, and the run is stopped by the field that follows it — the read-back cannot find the `Through` label because the app is no longer on screen. A focused re-check of the same entry, with the keyboard state printed at every step, showed the sequence working when it is driven alone (`tap → keyboard shown → type → Back → keyboard closed → the app still in the foreground`, `From` holds `2026-10-16`), so the defect is in the *state* the run reaches after the preceding steps, not in the app's field handling. The driver fix is: close the keyboard only while it is still shown, verify that it closed, and verify that the app is still in the foreground (relaunch it if not) before the next field is touched.
