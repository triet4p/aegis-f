### Outcome

- **Status:** `ready_for_review`
- **Observable result:**
  - **Data-first retrieval exists as a real tool, not a prompt rule.** `find_candidates(date_ref, end_date_ref, day_window, kinds, amount_ref, time_ref, cursor)` is a new read-only tool. It reads only this session's granted pending drafts (SQL filter on the granted id list, `PENDING`, and the effective stated day, where a user correction wins) and granted stored note readings (SQL filter on the granted capture ids and the day list). A day/month that states no year is searched across at most two trusted chronologies — the note's own recorded year and the run's current device year — both reported, instead of picking one. The window is capped at 31 calendar days and `day_window` at 3/3. Outcome is `NONE`/`ONE`/`MANY` plus `complete_within_scope`; rows carry the application's own `user_label`, a signed `day_offset`, the chronology label, exact amount agreement and an ordering-only minute distance. It cannot write, link, confirm, categorize or file anything.
  - **Read-only trusted current time.** `get_current_time()` returns device-local calendar components (year/month/day, hour/minute), weekday, timezone id and UTC offset, and a masked `today_ref` alias for the current day. It is the current-time reference only.
  - **Three chronologies, explicitly separated.** Current time (this tool), capture chronology (the capture's recorded instant + zone, used for its day words and a year-less date read from it), and answer time (the device-local year fixed when a `date` question was raised). `ask_user` snapshots the answer chronology on the run (`AgentToolSession.clarificationChronologies`, keyed by the subject handle) and `registerClarification` reads it back for the answer and for every continuation/retry of that same suspended run; a later clock or year cannot move the answer. A fresh replan fixes its own chronology because it re-reads current state.
  - **Spelled and natural temporal answers resolve locally.** `select_source` now returns `spelled_dates`: one masked date alias per spelled day/month inside the selection (`11 tháng 10`, `ngày 11 tháng 10 năm 2024`, English month names), minted as the date it stands for — the wording's own year, otherwise the capture's recorded local year, otherwise year-less. `registerClarification(field="date")` resolves a numeric date, a spelled date, an explicit year, or a natural year (`năm nay`, `năm ngoái`, `năm kia`, `năm sau`, English equivalents); a year-only answer is combined only with a day/month the subject already grounds inside its own span or stored reading. With no trusted year the answer stays unresolved with a specific, answerable note. Numeric ISO/slash behavior is unchanged (existing suite green).
  - **Privacy colors are preserved and deliberate.** Device clock facts cross as numeric calendar components (the criterion's own current-time requirement; numbers are not masked by `maskToolResultJson`). Stored/source-derived dates are never emitted as raw strings: a spelled answer or a selected spelled date becomes a vault **token** (masked alias), and the resolved year is reported only as a symbolic chronology label plus `year_resolved` — never as a number or ISO string. Candidate rows are value-free (no transaction date/time/amount/memo text). The new test asserts the tool result contains neither the stored ISO date, the amount digits, the stored time, nor the note text.
