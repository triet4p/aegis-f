## 3. Decisions and rationale to preserve

1. **`markExecuting` aligns `requestedQuestion` with the question the attempt really asks.** This
   was added in the S7.3 attempt because a row whose `requestedQuestion` was stale relative to the
   recomputed question (seeded/legacy row, or a job carried over from an older build) looked
   permanently out of date to its own completion: the settle saw `newerQuestion == true`,
   requeued with `attempts = 0`, and looped forever. Observed directly before the fix in the
   recreation test (repeated scheduling of the same draft, `CONTEXT_CHANGED` requeue, attempts
   reset every time). **Do not simply remove the alignment**; a correction must keep the
   stale-row/legacy case from looping *and* stop clobbering a newer retarget.
2. **`retarget` is the coalescing update for a running job.** It is documented as "the running
   attempt keeps its claim and finishes its own question, and the newer question runs next"; that
   promise is the one the race breaks.
3. **`newerQuestion` at settle is the single decision point** for requeue-vs-settle; any fix must
   leave that comparison meaningful (the row must still carry the newer requested question after
   `markExecuting`).
4. **One job per draft, one question per attempt, per-attempt idempotency keys.** The key now
   includes `ownerToken` (S7.4) — do not change key semantics in this correction.
5. **User decisions win.** `cancelForDraft`, `respectUserCancel`, resolved/dismissed guards and the
   "a fresh prediction is reused as RESULT without inference" admission path are established
   behavior; the correction must not disturb them.
6. **Scope discipline:** fix only the clobber; no redesign, no extra scenario work, no unrelated
   cleanup in the queue/DAO.
