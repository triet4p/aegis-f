## Detailed Evidence Assessment

### 1. Capture-Anchored Relative-Day Resolver Semantics

- **Closed, deterministic vocabulary:** In `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/agent/RelativeDayExpressions.kt:44-50`, expressions are bounded to exact single-day offsets:
  - Offset `0`: `today`, `ngày hôm nay`, `ngay hom nay`, `hôm nay`, `hom nay`
  - Offset `-1`: `yesterday`, `ngày hôm qua`, `ngay hom qua`, `hôm qua`, `hom qua`
  - Offset `-2`: `the day before yesterday`, `ngày hôm kia`, `ngay hom kia`, `hôm kia`, `hom kia`
  - Offset `1`: `tomorrow`, `ngày mai`, `ngay mai`
  - Offset `2`: `the day after tomorrow`, `ngày kia`, `ngay kia`, `ngày mốt`, `ngay mot`
- **Ambiguity preservation:** Period words (`last night`, `this morning`, `tonight`, `last week`, `this weekend`, `sáng nay`, `tối qua`, `đêm qua`, `chiều nay`, `tuần trước`) and bare words (`mai`, `mốt`, `qua`, `hôm nọ`) are excluded from `VOCABULARY`. They emit no alias, so the temporal anchor remains unresolved and the user is asked for an absolute date.
- **Lexical boundary & greedy overlap protection:** `BOUNDARY_BEFORE = "(?<![\\p{L}\\p{N}])"`, `BOUNDARY_AFTER = "(?![\\p{L}\\p{N}])"`. Matches sort by `start` asc, length desc; `candidate.start < consumedUntil continue` prevents sub-match overlap (e.g., "the day before yesterday" does not also match "yesterday").
- **Strict timezone handling:** In `RelativeDayExpressions.timeZone`, `runCatching { TimeZone.getTimeZone(timezoneId) }.getOrNull()?.takeIf { it.id == timezoneId }` ensures unknown or malformed timezone IDs (which Java's `TimeZone.getTimeZone` silently falls back to GMT for) return `null` instead of falling back to device or GMT time.
- **Capture-only anchoring:** In `AgentToolRuntime.captureReferenceDate`, the reference date is computed solely from `capture.capturedAtEpochMillis` and `capture.captureTimezone`. If either is missing or invalid, `resolveRelativeDay` throws `TEMPORAL_CONSTRAINT` (`capture_records_local_date_and_zone`), refusing to borrow device clock or another zone.
- **Calendar-day shift:** `RelativeDayExpressions.resolve` performs pure calendar-day arithmetic in UTC Calendar, correctly crossing month and year boundaries and rejecting impossible calendar dates (e.g., leap day in non-leap years).
