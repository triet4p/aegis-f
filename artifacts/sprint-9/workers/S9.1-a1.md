### Gap analysis (what was reused, what was missing)

Reused unchanged: durable captures with a recorded instant/zone, `note_events` per source span with
normalized text and optional amount/merchant/time, compare-and-set revisions, the closed eleven-tool
catalog and its per-intent subset, the executor's verified completion, the command boundary and the
inbound raw-value refusal.

Missing and delivered: a day stated **relative to the note** had no deterministic local resolution.
The S8 design recorded that as a separate future feature and the shipped skill told the model to ask
for an absolute date, so "yesterday" could not be anchored from the capture at all. S9.1's own text
requires exactly that ("use capture time/timezone for relative anchors").
