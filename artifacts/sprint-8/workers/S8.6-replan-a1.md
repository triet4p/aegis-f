- **Observable result:** new `io.github.triet4p.aegisf.privacy` package implements the mandatory local pre-egress boundary as the smallest maintainable deterministic regex approach from fixture evidence (no specialist model, no external masking service):
  - `SensitiveVault` — scoped reversible token mapping (per-session scope tag, typed `AMT`/`DATE`/`TIME`/`ACCT`/`BANK` namespaces, stable equality, local order/relative-time comparators, overlap/collision handling, cross-session isolation, bounded capacity with fail-closed `MaskingBlockedException`, redacted `toString`, no logging/serialization).
