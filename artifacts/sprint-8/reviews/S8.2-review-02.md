## 3. Acceptance Criteria and Contract Verification

### 3.1 Reopened Problem Context and Owner-Approved Rubric

During physical-device trials in S8.3, the real-effect contract discriminator (`artifacts/sprint-8/evidence/S8.3/a2/contract-discriminator/`) revealed that the initial evaluation contract's literal equality requirement was unachievable for valid models. The v1 contract compared tool call dictionaries by deep equality, failing functionally correct calls that produced the exact same real Room database effects solely because the model could not guess private test-fixture strings:
- literal `idempotencyKey` strings (e.g., `case-time-01` in 9 of 11 call-bearing cases);
- arbitrary fixture paraphrase strings in `interpretation.normalizedText` (e.g., `Book purchase` vs `Bought a book yesterday at 09:30` in 6 reading cases);
- unstated `extractionVersion` and `unresolvedReason` literal labels.

The project owner approved a versioned semantic scoring correction (`docs/sprint-plans/sprint-8.md:25-26`):
- Score the unknowable fields semantically: non-empty, unique, replay-evidenced idempotency keys; deterministic source-backed content matching the app-stored reading; exact unresolved decision with a well-formed label; declared producer provenance.
- Keep every tool name, sequence, scoped capture/event/draft ID, revision, source span, amount, merchant, date, time, interval, timezone, nullness, and caller authority boundary strictly exact.
- Keep cases, denominators, prompts, fixtures, and tool schemas immutable.
- Freeze a new versioned contract revision (`aegis-f-s8-workflow-scoring/v2` in `s8_workflow_scoring_v2.json`) before qualification trials.
- Preserve original v1 results and the v1 scoring engine (`score --scoring v1`); forbid retroactive qualification or PASS of earlier models.

### 3.4 Idempotency, Replay, and Stored Reading Semantics

1. **Idempotency Key Constraints:**
   - Keys must be strings <= 128 characters, non-empty, and free of ASCII control characters.
   - Keys must be unique within a case. Blank or whitespace-only keys are rejected (`idempotency_key_invalid`). Duplicate keys within a case are rejected (`idempotency_key_collision`).
   - The contract adheres to actual Android Room application rules: whitespace-padded keys are accepted as distinct valid keys, avoiding new unknowable format constraints.

2. **Replay Evidence Verification:**
   - Replay claims require real execution evidence: dispatch outcomes `["Applied", "NoChange"]`, exactly one durable `workflow_operations` row matching the idempotency key and command (`UPDATE_EVENT`, `PROPOSE_LINK`, `ATTACH_LINK`, `REQUEST_CLASSIFICATION`) with a terminal outcome, and identical before/after database state hashes (`state_sha256_before == state_sha256_after`).
   - Synthetic probes verify that retry duplication, fake marker-only replay without the durable row, or state mutations during replay are rejected.
   - Qualification requires one validated replay probe per profile; absent probes are reported as `missing` rather than inferred.

3. **Source-Backed Normalized Text & Stored Readings:**
   - Normalized text is validated through deterministic lower-bound constraints: length <= 200, no control characters, non-neutral token overlap with the source span or merchant, and every stated digit sequence must be accounted for by the span, the amount, or stated date/time components.
   - Fabricated numbers trigger `normalized_text_fabricated_facts`; nonsense text triggers `normalized_text_not_source_backed`.
   - The text must match the app-stored `note_events` row (`call_effects.stored_reading`). Missing stored readings or discrepancies trigger `stored_reading_evidence_missing` or `normalized_text_effect_mismatch`.

4. **Exact Boundaries & Authority Checks:**
   - All non-semantic fields (tool name, sequence, capture ID, event ID, revision, span offsets, amount, merchant, date, time) are compared strictly. Mutations in any of these fields fail with `call_intent_or_arguments_mismatch`.
   - Unknown tools and invalid schemas fail with `unknown_tool_call` and `call_schema_invalid`.
   - Clarification, refusal, and no-match intents require structured semantic targets matching expected scopes, non-empty text, and human review markers (`semantic_review == "pass"`), preventing substring loopholes.
