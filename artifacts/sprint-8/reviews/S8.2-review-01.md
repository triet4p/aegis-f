## 3. Acceptance Criteria and Contract Verification

### 3.1 Real S5 API Parity and Tool Mapping

The tool schema in `tools/slm/s8_workflow_tools.json` was audited against the live Kotlin service and entity contracts:

- **`query_draft_candidates`:** Maps to `WorkflowQueryService.draftCandidates(DraftCandidateQuery)`. Requires `query` containing `reviewStatus` (`PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `DISMISSED`, or `null`), `onlyPossibleDuplicates` (boolean), and `bounds` referencing `QueryPageRequest` (`limit` 1–500, `offset` >= 0). Matches `WorkflowQueryTypes.kt:16-36` and `246-250` exactly.
- **`update_event_interpretation`:** Maps to `WorkflowCommandService.updateEventInterpretation(scope, UpdateEventRequest)`. Validated against `WorkflowCommandTypes.kt:327-335` and `EventContextRepository.kt:21-36`. Enforces typed `spanStart` (>= 0), `spanEnd` (>= 1), `normalizedText`, optional `amountVnd`, `merchant`, and `timeAnchor` matching `NoteEventTimeAnchor` (`date`, `time`, `timezoneId`, `intervalEndDate`, `intervalEndTime`). Unresolved reasons like `TIME_NOT_STATED` are permitted without guessing.
- **`propose_link`:** Maps to `WorkflowCommandService.proposeLink(scope, ProposeLinkRequest)`. Requires `idempotencyKey`, `eventId`, and `draftId`. Link evidence is app-computed; caller-supplied evidence is disallowed.
- **`attach_link`:** Maps to `WorkflowCommandService.attachLink(scope, AttachLinkRequest)`. Requires `idempotencyKey`, `eventId`, `draftId`, `expectedRevision`, and optional `userOverride`. Gated by application evidence policy.
- **`request_laya_classification`:** Maps to `WorkflowCommandService.requestClassification(scope, ClassificationRequest)`. Uses `draftId`, exact review `description`, and `expectedReviewRevision`. Strictly review-only; cannot mutate ledger records.
- **Authority Boundary:** Trusted `WorkflowCallerScope` is intentionally omitted from all tool schemas. Scope is provided by host code, preventing model text from spoofing caller authority.
- **Forbidden Tools:** Destructive operations (`confirm_draft`, `delete_transaction`, `execute_sql`) are absent from the tool definition and forbidden by validator checks.

### 3.4 Scoring Architecture, Denominators, and Metric Contracts

The scoring rules in `s8_contract.py` and profile declarations in `s8_workflow_profiles.json` were audited:

- **Strict Denominators:** Denominators are fixed (10 frozen, 6 heldout). Missing results, parse errors, missing application effect traces, call argument mismatches, and semantic mismatches count as failures. Post-result case dropping is explicitly barred (`cases_may_be_dropped_after_results: false`).
- **Application Trace Requirement:** The scorer requires `effect_source: "instrumented_application"`; schema-valid candidate outputs without actual application traces fail scoring.
- **Clarification & Refusal Grading:** Replaces defective substring-search graders with structured semantic targets and reviewed responses (`mustAsk` and `scope`).
- **Telemetry & Resource Metrics:** Enforces explicit tracking of first-result latency (cold/warm median and p95), cold-start duration, prefill tokens/s, decode tokens/s, peak process RSS vs PSS (in MiB), device `MemAvailable` baseline/min (in KiB), and cancellation latency with applied effect reporting.
- **Proposed Comparison Targets:** The proposed targets (80% frozen / 70% held-out correctness, 30 s first-result, 60 s cold start, 1400 MiB RSS) are explicitly marked `proposed_only: true` and `owner_approved_acceptance: false`. They are comparison baselines and not silent acceptance gates.
