## 5. Root cause and actionable evidence

Observed, with exact locations:

- The assisted record builder reads resources that `runCase` already closed: `S813QualificationInstrumentedTest.kt:1183-1184` (`laya.close(); db.close()`) runs before the assisted step, while the continuation runner calls `postconditions(db, …)` at `:1152` and `layaEvidence(…, db, …)` at `:1160`. `postconditions` is a suspend DAO reader at `:1606`.
- The assisted step is outside the per-case error boundary: the try/catch covers `runCase` at `:812`, but `runAssistedStep(...)` is called at `:858` and is not guarded. Any throwable there escapes the loop and cancels the batch.
- The failure surfaced immediately after the 4th HTTP 200 and before the phase turn record, consistent with the first closed-resource DAO read in record construction. No app-level stack was emitted, so the exact owning scope of the cancelled `SupervisorJobImpl` is not provable from the raw log; this is labelled inference in the evidence file.

Rejected non-fix (per Main): catching `Throwable` around the assisted step merely to suppress the cancellation.
