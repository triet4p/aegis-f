## Result

The home screen now opens with a compact **THIS MONTH** card. It names the device-local calendar month (`October 2026` on this host) and shows that month's confirmed spending in exact whole dong, plus one row per category that has spending — `No category` for unfiled spending and `<name> (archived)` for a category archived after its records were filed. It is reading the same live Room streams as the rest of the app, so a save, correction, archive, or deletion moves the figure on its own.

Only records that are `EXPENSE`, `CONFIRMED`, and inside `[local start of the current month, local start of the next month)` are counted. Income and internal transfers never inflate spending, a draft is not spending, a row in the neighbouring month belongs to that month, and a deleted row is absent from the stream. Money is summed with the existing `VndMoney.checkedAddToTotal`; a total past `Long.MAX_VALUE` is shown as a labeled lower bound (`… or more`, with an explanatory line in the error color) instead of wrapping, being invented, or crashing.

The card lives on the home screen, deliberately **not** on the browsing list: the Saved-transactions filters are browsing state on another screen and cannot read or write anything here, so the month figure can never be silently narrowed to a filtered subset, and the list viewport that S2.6-a3 just un-cramped is untouched. The card is one compact surface on a home screen that already scrolls; it stores nothing and adds no second ledger.

## Lifecycle, resume, and timezone behaviour (partly observed; the rest reasoned from the source)

Observed on the host: the launcher smoke's month came from the device clock at composition — `SMOKE month=October 2026 monthStart=1790787600000 monthEnd=1793466000000`, i.e. local midnight on 1 October and on 1 November in the host zone — and after navigating into `Saved transactions` and pressing `Back` the panel showed the month figure again, which is the re-entry path working.

Reasoned from the source (no device, clock jump, or zone change was exercised by this attempt):

- **Re-entering the screen, a configuration change, or a new process**: `nowEpochMillis` starts from `System.currentTimeMillis()` inside the composable, and the bounds and the month label are derived from it, so every *fresh* composition recomputes the current month in the current `TimeZone.getDefault()`. A new process additionally re-reads the same database file, which is why the figure survives a restart rather than being cached anywhere.
- **Staying on the home screen across the first midnight of a month**: a `LaunchedEffect` sleeps until `nextMonthStartEpochMillis(now) - now` and then re-reads the clock, so the panel moves to the new month without a restart. That delay is dispatched by the main-thread coroutine dispatcher, whose timed dispatch is measured in Android `uptimeMillis`, so a boundary crossed while the device is in deep sleep (screen off, dozing) can fire late; the panel then updates when that timer resumes, when the screen is composed again, or when a record is saved (the flows re-emit and the panel recomposes). A plain recomposition while the screen stays composed deliberately does **not** re-read the clock — the timer owns the boundary.
- **A timezone change while the panel stays composed**: every conversion reads `TimeZone.getDefault()` at the moment it runs, so the next fresh composition uses the new zone; nothing in the panel *listens* for a zone change, so a panel already on screen keeps the bounds it computed in the previous zone until then. Not exercised, and not required by the S2.7 contract.
- Not exercised anywhere: a real device dozing across a month boundary, a runtime timezone change, and a genuine process kill — the host smoke proved in-process recomposition only, and the checklist's swipe step is a close/reopen check, not a guaranteed process death.

## Decisions

- The summary is on the **home screen**, not on the browsing list. The requirement that it must not be silently redefined by browsing filters is then structural rather than defensive, and the list viewport that S2.6-a3 had just fixed (filters collapsed, results owning nearly the whole screen) is untouched by this task.
- The month is computed once in `ManualDateTime`, next to the app's other local-date conversions, instead of inside the composable: the boundaries are then testable without a clock, and there is still exactly one definition of "local midnight" and "one month later".
- Overflow is reported with a proven bound (`Long.MAX_VALUE`, `or more`, plus an explanatory line) rather than a partial sum: the partial depends on the order Room happens to emit rows, while `Long.MAX_VALUE` is a true lower bound for any order, and neither wraps nor invents a figure.
- One emitted flow pair drives both the total and the labels, so the summary cannot show a total for a category the screen has not loaded yet.
- No permanent test was added for the panel's rendering: a rendered-text test would keep the Compose UI-test dependencies in the build permanently for a check whose subject — the derived total and breakdown — the permanent tests already cover directly, and the assignment called for one focused host UI check rather than a cosmetic matrix. The throwaway smoke covered the wiring once, over the real launcher and database.
