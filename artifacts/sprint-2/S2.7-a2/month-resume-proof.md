## Result

The home screen's **THIS MONTH** card now reports the month the device is in *now*, including when the app was backgrounded and resumed without being closed:

- The clock reading and the device zone are read **together**, once, into one snapshot that carries the month's half-open bounds and its heading, so the label, the range, and the rows can never come from different readings.
- The snapshot is re-taken on `ON_RESUME` (the panel/activity coming back to the foreground) and at the next month boundary. A device that spent time in the background while the calendar crossed into a new month, or while the device zone moved, therefore reports the month it is actually in instead of the figures it computed before the pause.
- The zone is part of the snapshot key, not only the clock reading: a device that changed zone while the app was away is picked up even when the clock reading happens to be identical.
- Initial load is unchanged (the same read at first composition), the boundary timer is kept, and a resume that changes neither month nor zone is a no-op: the same figures stay on screen with no re-query and no blanking.

Aggregation, overflow reporting, the category breakdown, the empty state, the inflated-by-income/transfer/draft exclusion, and the location of the card on the home screen are untouched.

## Decisions

- **One snapshot instead of re-deriving each piece.** Bounds and heading now come out of a single read, so a resume can never produce a card whose heading and range disagree, and the zone is part of what changes the snapshot (a phone that moved zone while the app was away is picked up even if the millisecond reading is identical — the case the previous code could not have handled even with a resume re-read keyed on the clock alone).
- **The rerun key is the snapshot, not a counter.** `LaunchedEffect(shownMonth)` re-arms the boundary wait after every read, including the one a resume performs, so a background pause can no longer leave a timer counting toward a boundary that has already passed; when a resume changes nothing, structural equality means no recomposition and no restart.
- **The resume hook lives in the panel, not the activity.** The month is the panel's own state; adding an activity-level callback would spread one concern across two files, and the home screen is the only place that reads it.
- **No production clock seam for testability.** The smoke drove the month through the device zone instead of adding a clock parameter to the panel: the shipped code stays exactly the code that was smoked, and the un-exercised clock input is reported instead of engineered around.
