## Actionable findings

1. **MEDIUM — Guard registration can overwrite a newer owner, leaving a duplicate-classification window.** `WorkflowClaimGuard.hold` unconditionally assigns `held[key] = ownerToken` (`app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/data/WorkflowClaimGuard.kt:33-35`). A newly inserted claim is not registered in that guard until after the database insert (`app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/data/WorkflowCommandService.kt:979-998`); takeover likewise updates the durable row before registering the new token (`:1057-1068`). If the original claim holder is paused after its insert long enough for its lease to expire, a second service instance can see no guard, take over the row, register its token, and start classification. When the first holder resumes, its unconditional `hold` replaces the newer token and it proceeds into `runClaimedClassification`/`runModelWork` (`:636-687, 864-895`). Both attempts can then invoke the classifier. The durable owner fence still prevents the old attempt from committing a stale result, but it does not prevent this duplicate model call; the r2 invariant is therefore not closed for this acquisition/takeover interleaving. **Confidence: high.**
