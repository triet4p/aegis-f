## 1. Executive Summary & Review Scope

Task S10.3 implements the core financial viewing, analytics, and browsing surfaces for Aegis, delivering on the owner-approved S10.1 single-task-per-screen architecture and anti-stacking philosophy:

- **Focused Home (`HomeScreen`):** Serves strictly as a task launcher, not a stacked dashboard. Features a time-of-day greeting, a Review bell with live pending draft count, a single compact spending glance with privacy masking (`••• ••• ₫`), and eight labelled feature launchers (`Ledger`, `Analytics`, `Notes`, `AI Chat`, `Review`, `Manual Record`, `Categories & Rules`, `Settings`) positioned directly on the surface without enclosing container cards.
- **Analytics & Reports (`AnalyticsScreen`):** Dedicated analytics destination. Calendar month navigation chevrons walk full months with exact dates and day counts, alongside a custom inclusive range selector. Four distinct exact statements are presented: confirmed spending, confirmed income, net cash flow (income minus spending, internal transfers strictly excluded), and internal transfers. A proportional category breakdown bar and list drill directly into scoped spending records, accompanied by a daily spending bar chart with an accessible text-list alternative. Unconfirmed drafts never enter any figure.
- **Scoped Drill & Restoration:** An Analytics drill route carries its own period and category snapshot, allowing deep navigation into scoped ledger and receipt detail while guaranteeing that Back returns to the identical period, figures, and scroll position.
- **Ledger Browsing (`LedgerScreen`):** Owns record browsing with literal substring description search (SQLite `instr`, where `%` and `_` are treated strictly as user-typed characters), type filter chips, collapsible category and date filter panel, device-local calendar day grouping, and bounded 500-row page controls.
- **Digital Receipt (`TransactionDetailScreen`):** Displays exact signed amount, confirmation status, transaction type, category (with archived indicator if applicable), device-local timestamp, provenance source, note, record id, and recorded/corrected timestamps. Prefilled Edit and guarded Delete (requiring explicit naming confirmation) are anchored at the bottom; stale records detect revision mismatch and reload rather than deleting unseen state.
- **Retirement of Obsolete Presentation:** Retires legacy `DashboardScreen`, `TransactionListScreen`, and `HomeLauncherScreen` cleanly without dead routes or compatibility shims.

In Round 1 (`S10.3-review-a1`), three visual/layout defects across different text scales were identified (and proactively disclosed by worker `S103Implementation`), resulting in a consolidated `changes_requested` verdict (`advance: false`). Main also directed that read-error handling be proven via runtime evidence at the UI boundary rather than source-only Flow `.catch` audit.

In Round 2, worker `S103Implementation` submitted Candidate 2:
1. Resolved all three layout defects (reduced KPI amount style to `titleSmall` to eliminate digit wrapping at 1.0×; restructured `TransactionRow` to title+amount on line 1 and full-width `category · time · source` on line 2; stacked receipt actions vertically for text scales $\ge 1.3\times$).
2. Added focused runtime instrumentation tests in `S103BrowsingLayoutInstrumentedTest` utilizing an in-memory database with a dropped `transactions` table to prove real query failure handling and recovery UI ("The stored records could not be read. Nothing was changed." + "Try again") on both `AnalyticsScreen` and `TransactionDetailScreen`.
3. Verified all fixes on the physical HONOR ALT-LX2 device (Android 14 / API 34), with 605 passing host tests, 8 passing S10.3 layout instrumented tests, and retained-shell regression suites passing. Production database state was fully reconciled and cleanly restored to baseline.

Zero actionable findings remain. Round 2 concludes with **PASS** (`advance: true`).

## 3. Audit of Round-1 Findings & Bounded Fix Resolution

### 3.1. Finding 1: Reports Income KPI Card Wraps Inside Digits at 1.0× Scale (MEDIUM) — RESOLVED

- **Files / Lines:** `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/ui/AnalyticsScreen.kt:469, 487`
- **Initial Defect:** In 50%-width side-by-side KPI cards at 1.0× font scale, `headlineSmall` text caused large dong amounts such as `+28,500,000 ₫` to wrap across two lines inside the digits (`+28,500,00` on line 1, `0 ₫` on line 2), as captured in `device/100-analytics-figures.png` and `device/132-analytics-vi.png`.
- **Correction Applied:** Updated amount typography style from `headlineSmall` to `titleSmall` in `KpiSection`.
- **Verification:** Physical phone captures `device/200-analytics-kpi-en-1.0.png` (English) and `device/202-analytics-kpi-vi-1.0.png` (Vietnamese) confirm that `+28,500,000 ₫` renders entirely on a single line within the card at 1.0× scale, with vertical card stacking retained for $\ge 1.3\times$ text scale.

### 3.2. Finding 2: Ledger Row Category Label Crushed to Single Letters Under Content Pressure (MEDIUM) — RESOLVED

- **Files / Lines:** `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/ui/LedgerScreen.kt:785-821`
- **Initial Defect:** `TransactionRow` placed category name and time/source metadata on the same horizontal row alongside a right-aligned amount column. At 1.5× scale, "No category" was compressed to `N...` (`ledger-page-2-1.5x.png`), and at 1.0× scale "S103 Shopping" was truncated to `S103 Shop...` (`110-ledger-root.png`).
- **Correction Applied:** Restructured `TransactionRow` into a 2-line layout: line 1 displays description (`weight(1f)`) and amount (`TextAlign.End`), while line 2 gives full width to `categoryName · time · source` with `maxLines = 2` and `TextOverflow.Ellipsis`.
- **Verification:** Physical device captures `device/201-ledger-row-en-1.0.png` and `device/203-ledger-row-vi-1.0.png` at 1.0×, and instrumentation capture `instrumentation/ledger-page-2-1.5x.png` at 1.5×, verify that category names (`S103 Shopping`, `No category`) and full provenance metadata render cleanly without premature truncation.

### 3.3. Finding 3: Receipt Detail Anchored Action Buttons Break Words Mid-Word at 1.5× Scale (LOW/MEDIUM) — RESOLVED

- **Files / Lines:** `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/ui/TransactionDetailScreen.kt:423-458, 474, 494`
- **Initial Defect:** Side-by-side buttons in `Row(weight(1f))` caused words to break mid-word without hyphens at 1.5× scale (`Edit transacti/on`, `Delete × transac/tion`), as captured in `instrumentation/receipt-1.5x.png` and `receipt-stale-1.5x.png`.
- **Correction Applied:** `DetailActions` now detects `LocalDensity.current.fontScale >= 1.3f` and stacks `EditAction` and `DeleteAction` vertically in a `Column` with full width and explicit `defaultMinSize(minHeight = 48.dp)`.
- **Verification:** Updated instrumentation captures `receipt-1.5x.png` and `receipt-stale-1.5x.png` show full-width stacked buttons with clean, unbroken labels (`Edit transaction` and `× Delete transaction`), satisfying the 48dp minimum touch target requirement.

### 3.4. Runtime Read-Error Evidence (Main Directive) — VERIFIED

- **Files / Lines:** `app/src/androidTest/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/ui/S103BrowsingLayoutInstrumentedTest.kt:441-517, 555-562`
- **Requirement:** Main directed that source-only audit of Flow `.catch` blocks is insufficient; safe focused runtime evidence using controlled read failure at the UI boundary was required.
- **Implementation:** Added helper `failingDatabase()` which constructs a separate in-memory `LedgerDatabase` and drops the `transactions` table (`DROP TABLE IF EXISTS transactions`). Two dedicated tests (`analyticsReadErrorShowsRecoveryAtTheUiBoundary` and `receiptReadErrorShowsRecoveryAtTheUiBoundary`) bind the real screens to services on this failing database.
- **Verification:** Both tests pass and capture rendered recovery UI:
  - `instrumentation/analytics-read-error-1.5x.png`: Displays error card "The stored records could not be read. Nothing was changed." and "Try again" action button.
  - `instrumentation/receipt-read-error-1.5x.png`: Displays error title "Couldn't read this transaction", body "The stored record could not be read. Nothing was changed.", and "Try again" action button.
