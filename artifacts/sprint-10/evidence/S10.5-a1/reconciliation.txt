## After safe verification

The candidate APK and the instrumentation APK were installed with `adb install -r` only; the app
was never cleared or uninstalled. Production preferences were returned to exactly `en` / `system`
and re-read from `aegis_ui_preferences.xml` after a force-stop/relaunch:

- transactions = 0 (unchanged)
- categories = 0 (unchanged)
- pending_bank_drafts = 8 (2 new PENDING rows, ids 7 and 8)
- capture_messages = 6 (the two matching BANK_EVIDENCE_REF rows for drafts 7 and 8)
- note_events = 2 (unchanged)
- context_links = 0 (unchanged)
- workflow_operations = 7 (unchanged)
- agent_runs = 0 (unchanged; no hosted request was sent)
- agent_conversation = 0 (unchanged)
- agent_trace_events = 0 (unchanged)
- remembered_category_rules = 0 (unchanged)
- app preferences: language = en, theme_mode = system

The two additional pending drafts and their capture evidence are ordinary bank-notification
intake by the app's existing reconciliation when the activity resumed during verification. They
are the owner's real notifications, not S10.5 fixtures. Their amount text, account text and memo
are not copied into this evidence. No row was deleted or edited by hand, no draft was confirmed or
dismissed, no transaction/category/note/link changed, and no owner data was reconstructed.

## Hosted egress

No production hosted request was made by the worker. The app has no saved provider profile
(Settings shows only preset choices; `activeProvider()` is null). Owner permission for S10.5
verification is recorded by Main, but permission is not a credential: no usable credential exists
in the app, so the mandatory real-provider criterion is unresolved. No credential, endpoint
secret, profile payload or provider request/response body was read, copied, logged or recorded.
The scripted instrumentation adapter runs with an isolated in-memory DB and never opens a network
connection; the opt-in real-provider test asserts the absence of a profile and skips.
