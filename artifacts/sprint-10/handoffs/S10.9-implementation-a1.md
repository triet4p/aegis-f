## Decisions and rationale

1. **Isolated package instead of the owner app.** Added `.s109evidence` to the existing suffix
   allow-list so every write and screenshot stayed out of `io.github.triet4p.aegisf`.
2. **Real shell under test.** The cross-flow suite mounts the production `AegisAppShell` with the
   production screens and commands over a fresh in-memory Room database; only the activity content
   is replaced. This exercises real route stacks, saveable state and insets without seeding the
   owner.
3. **Three observed defects were fixed, not just recorded.** The review-empty duplication and
   pager, the clipped attached-note context, and the invisible draft-confirm validation are user
   visible; each received the smallest local fix. The snackbar was preferred over scrolling to an
   inline message because the action is sticky and may be tapped from any scroll position.
4. **No system/radio changes.** Unlike earlier tasks, heads-up notification suppression was not
   used. Two captures that briefly included a personal Messenger heads-up were deleted at once;
   the retained set has no personal content. Airplane mode, Wi-Fi, data, font scale, permissions
   and owner data were untouched.
5. **Test-only tags.** `ledger-search` and `note-composer` are stable handles for verification;
   they alter no layout or behavior.
6. **Chat proof reuse.** The S10.5 chat suite already covers lifecycle states, offline/no-provider
   behavior, Stop/late-chunk invariants, link cards, 1.5× composer and real authorized streaming.
   The plan was to re-run its focused methods in the S10.9 package (hence the package-guard
   generalization) rather than duplicate 1,600 lines of tests.
