## Goal and acceptance

Local note entry has one primary action, Save locally. The saved list is a focused browse child;
one note's detail, edit/delete and source-backed multi-event editing open focused children/sheets,
never a composer stack. The selected note hands off explicitly to Chat with a visible removable
context; nothing auto-sends. Source/time/provenance, independent event access and the manual
handoff are retained. No placeholders or fake persistence/context. Design follows the S10.1
tokens/hierarchy and Main's final focused-root direction rather than the illustrative render's
composer+inline list+banner composition.
