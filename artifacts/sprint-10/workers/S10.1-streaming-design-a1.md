## 1. Executive Summary & Refined Scope

Task S10.1 delivers the complete mobile UI/UX redesign for Aegis, replacing the legacy endless vertical card dashboard with a modern, task-focused mobile architecture inspired by market benchmarks (**VietinBank iPay Mobile 6.x**, **MoMo Super-App**, **Revolut**, **Monzo**).

Following the technical PASS of cycle `S10.1-cycle-1`, the product owner introduced two authoritative refinements for the mobile experience:
1. **Dedicated Streaming AI Chat Screen:**
   > *"Chỗ AI nên có màn hình để streaming chat"*
   A dedicated, discoverable conversation screen with incrementally streamed assistant replies, clear lifecycle states (waiting, streaming, tool-working, completed, stopped, error), composer/Send/Stop behavior, in-context draft link actions, conversation history matching Room entities, and decoupling of quick notes from AI.
2. **Anti-Stacking & Single-Task Ergonomic Principle:**
   > *"Mới lại hạn chế việc stack các khối component trong cùng 1 màn hình. Cố gắng mỗi màn nên chỉ đảm nhiệm 1 nhiệm vụ nào đó xác định"*
   Every screen must have **ONE explicit purpose and ONE primary action (CTA)**. Secondary workflows, deep edits, or multi-item feeds are delegated to dedicated child screens or contextual sheets. Information density is permitted strictly when presenting data serving the *same* task (e.g. digital receipt breakdown, KPI analytics comparison), never as an endless vertical pile of heterogeneous functional launchers.

As `S101ChatDesigner`, all acceptance criteria and owner refinements were implemented, verified via automated headless Chromium browser testing, and captured across high-definition static renders.

## 3. Dedicated Streaming AI Chat Screen Design & Contracts

### 3.1 Visual Components & Top Bar
- **Top App Bar:** Back button `←`, screen title (*"Trợ lý AI"* / *"AI Assistant"*), model preset indicator (*"Mô hình: gpt-4o-mini"*), signature synthetic badge (`🔶 SYNTHETIC STREAM`), conversation history shortcut icon, and clear chat action.
- **Explicit Attached Context Chip:** When navigated from Quick Capture or Draft Detail, an explicit dismissible context chip is anchored above the transcript:
  `[📝 Ngữ cảnh đính kèm: "Trưa nay ăn phở Thìn 65k..."  ✕ Bỏ]`
  Any context egressed to the model is 100% transparent and user-controllable.

### 3.2 Incremental Token Streaming & State Lifecycle
- **Local Chunk Timer Simulation:** Driven by an interactive chunk sequence at ~180ms intervals, truthfully demonstrating incremental Server-Sent Events decoding (`HostedSseDecoder`) without fake backend calls.
- **All 6 Explicit States Supported:**
  1. *Waiting (Pending):* `"⏳ Đang kết nối mô hình…"` (pulsing spinner).
  2. *Streaming (Running):* Incrementally appended visible response text with blinking typing caret `▍`.
  3. *Tool-Working:* `"🔍 Đang đối chiếu biến động ngân hàng..."` (user-friendly tool status; no raw tool args, JSON schemas, tokens, or hidden reasoning exposed).
  4. *Completed (Succeeded):* Complete response rendered, typing caret removed, in-context `LinkEvidenceCard` rendered.
  5. *Stopped (Cancelled):* Immediate halt of chunking timer, partial response text preserved in transcript with `"⏹ Đã dừng phản hồi bởi người dùng"`. Zero late chunks leak after stop.
  6. *Error (Failed):* Inline retry banner with `"⚠️ Không thể hoàn thành phản hồi"` and `[Thử lại]` CTA.

### 3.3 Stop Invariant & Zero Late Chunks
- While generating, the composer Send button transforms into an active red square **`[Dừng / Stop]`** button.
- Tapping Stop clears the timer (`clearInterval`) immediately.
- Displayed partial content received prior to cancellation is committed to the conversation transcript as a stopped turn; already verified link effects or state mutations remain intact.
- No late chunks arrive after stop.
- The composer immediately restores to Send mode for the next message.

### 3.4 Scrolling Ergonomics & Keyboard Insets
- Auto-scroll follows stream when the user is scrolled to the bottom.
- If the user scrolls up to inspect previous turns, auto-scroll is paused to prevent disorienting jumps.
- A floating `[Cuộn xuống mới nhất ↓]` button appears when scrolled up, allowing one-tap return to the live stream.
- Anchored above the software keyboard (`WindowInsets.imePadding`).

### 3.5 In-Context Action Cards & Financial Confirmation Boundary
- When the AI proposes a link, a `LinkEvidenceCard` renders directly within the conversation stream:
  * Bank draft counterparty, formatted VND amount, timestamp.
  * Match rationale (*"Khớp số tiền 150.000 ₫ và mốc thời gian 14:15 với ghi chú"*).
  * Explicit actions: `[✓ Gắn kết (Attach)]` and `[✕ Từ chối (Reject)]`.
  * Truthful boundary notice: *"Xác nhận tài chính vào sổ quỹ chỉ diễn ra khi bạn nhấn Xác nhận tại màn hình Duyệt biến động"*. The AI assistant cannot autonomously execute financial transactions or alter ledger balances.

### 3.6 Conversation History Design (Durable vs. Transient Alignment)
- **Durable Memory (`AgentConversationEntity`, `AgentRunEntity`):** Persisted in SQLite via Room. Stores the sequence of user-visible turns: the user's request words (`USER`), lifecycle status lines (`STATUS`), and masked-safe terminal or clarification answers (`AGENT`). Durable rows carry no credentials, vault mappings, transient reasoning, or provider wire tokens.
- **Transient State (`ChatAgentRunState`):** Owned by the UI/shell during the active process lifecycle. Manages the active coroutine `Job`, live streaming text buffer (`streamText`), in-progress tool progress string (`progress`), and temporary continuation token. On process recreation or tab re-entry, the UI gracefully re-reads the durable conversation rows without attempting ungrounded token auto-resume.
