### Finding corrected

The differential review (`artifacts/sprint-10/reviews/S10.11-UI-differential-01.md` §3 finding 1)
found that the worker record's observable-result sentence —

> "Reasoning text is never displayed, stored or traced"

— and the R2 report's blanket wording ("never displayed, stored, traced, or reconstructed") assert
a system-wide property contradicted by the slice's own evidence. Read-only factcheck confirmed:

- `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/agent/AgentTraceRecorder.kt:129-144` —
  `providerStreamChunk` stores each raw SSE event's `data` text verbatim.
- `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/agent/TracingHttpTransport.kt:79-81` — every
  `HostedStreamFrame.Event` is passed to `recorder.providerStreamChunk` before being emitted.
- The Chat Completions SSE delta payload field `reasoning_content` therefore persists in the local
  private `agent_trace_events` store; the slice's own delta counts were extracted from those stored
  payloads (`verification.md` §1 and §4).
- `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/agent/AgentRunHistory.kt:484-492` — the trace is a
  separate private store and is not part of `safeExport`.
- `app/src/main/java/io/github/triet4p/aegisf/provider/OpenAiChatCompletionsAdapter.kt:207-218,
  711-716` — the adapter reports reasoning presence only and never reads the text into the answer,
  trace or continuation, so the adapter-level statement is accurate; only the unqualified
  system-level wording was wrong.
